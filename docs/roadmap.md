# Roadmap

Cosmonautics is in early access. This page separates playable release content from destinations retained for future development.

## Available now

- Earth-based progression, Mission Control, propellants, and launch-site construction
- Direct Artemis travel between Earth and the Moon
- Lunar pressure, oxygen, habitats, catacombs, resources, and settlement
- Station construction, EVA, docking, asteroid processing, and gas production
- Lunar Scrap → Lunar Alloy Solution → Lunar Alloy production
- Player-owned Solo and Duo Lunar Rovers with Hydrogen fuel
- Interactive Launch Site, Survive in Space, Space Station, and Moon workshop tutorials
- The Space Program advancement journey

## Coming soon

The interplanetary expansion is unreleased. Its destinations and travel systems are unavailable in beta.7 release builds:

- Mars
- Europa
- Asteroid Belt
- Vulcanus
- Moving Station voyages, including Earth–Moon Station travel
- Station thrusters and Thruster Assembly
- The solar-system Dynmap view

Their designs and availability may change while development continues. Configuration, debug mode, operator permissions, and commands cannot unlock this content in a release build.
